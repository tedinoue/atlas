## Notes
- Human profile was explicitly CORRECTED downward during profiling: "Our initial ratings systematically overscored the human. We scored peak performance rather than typical performance" (Part 2)
- Novel problem solving was one of the key corrections: downgraded from Exceptional to Strong
- Social modeling was corrected: downgraded from Exceptional to Strong (cross-cultural weakness)
- The human shows "the widest malleability range" — multiple dimensions shift 1-2 full points with training
- Processing scale (1) is "the most conspicuous valley on the chart"
- "humans have the most complete cognitive skill set of the three entities profiled" — broadest coverage, moderate or above on 20 of 21 dimensions
