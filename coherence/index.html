Overview: The Coherence Gradient

Multimodal AI models (GPT-4o, Claude 3, Gemini) enforce semantic coherence when visual input contradicts strong priors (e.g., a pink stop sign). This application maps the four primary AI resolution strategies observed in experiments to established human perceptual phenomena, exploring the literature across predictive coding, schema theory, and confabulation.

VLM Conflict Resolution Strategies

  • Silent Override: Reporting expected color (calling orange banana "yellow").
  • Gravitational Distortion: Pulling report toward expectation (pink stop sign called "red").
  • Narrative Construction: Confabulating explanations ("digital placeholder").
  • Selective Attention Failure: Omitting the anomalous object entirely.

The Gradient of Mismatch Resolution

As semantic violation magnitude increases, both biological and artificial networks shift strategies to maintain coherence.
